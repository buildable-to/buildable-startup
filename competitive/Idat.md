# Idat (Idat.de) — Competitive Teardown

*Status: evaluated*

## Overview
- **Funding:** Private company, owned by it's founders
- **Approach:** Revit pluggin, not independent tool
- **Target:** Companies using Revit

## First-Hand Evaluation
- [x] Did Demo as potential customer
- [x] Tested structural element generation
- [x] Documented output quality
- [x] Followed on Linkedin
- [ ] Captured screenshots

## What They Do Well
Detailing in 3D model and automatic generation of 2D shop drawings. integrated into Revit and user who already work there don't have to change platform. 

## What They Do Poorly
Their program is not 100% accurat. while showing demo their program made error doing detailing in 3D model and as we know from engineers shop drawings are not 100% accurat and they still have to work to make it 100%.

## Pricing
Subscription based model per user. 
Standard-3250Euro/user/year +Required Revit subscription - $3000/year/user; Standard price decreases if company purchases it for few or more engineers. 

## Could They Pivot to AI Text-to-CAD?
Unlike short-term. Creating AI tool kills their core product -Revit pluggin and changes their current approaches completely. Also they are explanding inside precast industry and creating solutions for other workflows, but they still might do it in long-term and their main advatage is built distribution. 

## Key Takeaway
They told me that their program is 100% accurat, but as I saw in the demo and as one engineer told us that is a lie. Also it takes 5 days of training +2-3 weeks (with 9am to 6pm intensity) for configuration to start using it effectively. 
They created program which is useful, but they reached their limit and could not achieve 100% accuracy with their program.
